# Project Overview: CoffeeShop E-commerce

## Concept & Style
- **Theme:** Cozy coffee shop, modern minimalism, warm and inviting.
- **Style:** Clean UI, soft shadows, rounded corners, smooth interactions.
- **Inspiration:** Starbucks, high-end coffee brands, modern SaaS dashboards.

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** #6F4E37 (Coffee Brown)
- **Secondary:** #C4A484 (Latte Beige)
- **Accent:** #D2691E (Caramel Orange)
- **Background:** #F5F5DC (Cream / Light Beige)
- **Card BG:** #FFFFFF (White)
- **Text Primary:** #2E2E2E (Dark Gray)
- **Text Secondary:** #7A7A7A (Soft Gray)
- **Border:** #E0E0E0 (Light Gray)
- **Success:** #4CAF50
- **Error:** #E53935

## Typography
- **Font Family:** "Poppins", "Roboto", sans-serif.
- **Heading:** Bold, slightly rounded.
- **Body:** Clean and readable.
- **Buttons:** Medium weight.

## Logo Design
- **Text:** "CoffeeShop"
- **Style:** Minimal, modern.
- **Icon:** Coffee cup or coffee bean.
- **Colors:** #6F4E37 + #D2691E.

## Key Features & Screens
### Customer Side
- **Homepage:** Hero banner, featured products, categories, promotions.
- **Product List:** Grid layout, product cards with "Add to cart".
- **Product Detail:** Large image, description, quantity selector, CTAs.
- **Cart & Checkout:** Order summary, coupons, payment (COD, VNPAY).
- **Search:** Suggestion dropdown.
- **Profile:** Order history, user info.
- **Auth:** Login/Register forms.

### Admin Dashboard
- **Overview:** Revenue charts, order summary.
- **Management:** Products, Categories, Users, Orders, Coupons (Table + CRUD).

## UI/UX Principles
- **Components:** 8px rounded buttons with soft shadows, 6px border-radius inputs.
- **UX:** Simple navigation, fast checkout, clear CTAs, mobile-responsive, accessible.
- **Extras:** Micro-interactions, loading skeletons, toast notifications.
